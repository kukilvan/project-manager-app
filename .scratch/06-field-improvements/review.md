## Findings

- **F1** — Hebrew word for the pull. The plan legend and the Hebrew «PDF с номерами» legend of ticket 6.8 used משיכה / נמשכה; the rest of the app, the management sheet (הושחל) and spec § 5.1 use השחלה. Worse, «протянута» was also in `15-planversion.json` with «הושחלה», which the build keeps, so the in-app legend mixed «הושחלה» with «משיכה» in one line. The same old word was in one line of the drawing-version screen («בדוחות המשיכה…»). **Fix now:** השחלה in `PLAN_LEGEND`, the PDF legend line of `src/tohnit/numbered.js`, the plan legend and guide line in `07-plan.json`, the line in `15-planversion.json`; `NUMBERED_V` 3 → 4 so the PDFs on Drive get the corrected Hebrew legend once.
- **F2** — `scripts/i18n-build.mjs` keeps the last file's entry when the same Russian text is in two source files; 18 texts had two different translations, so an edit of the earlier one never reached the screen (that is how F1's «נמשכה» stayed unseen). Eight of them came with ticket 6.7 (`19-rest.json`), one with ticket 6.8 (F1's «протянута»), nine are older. **Fix now:** the overridden entries removed (the built `en.json` and `he.json` have exactly the same translations as before, compared key by key), and a test in `tests/i18n.test.js` fails on a second, different translation. Identical duplicates (17) are harmless and stay.
- **F3** — Guide line about the «Проблема» column (ticket 6.4): it sent the person to «Ещё → Таблицы для руководства → столбцы», which does not exist (the screen is «Для руководства», the list «Столбцы» is in the form behind «✏️ Изменить»), and it said the row turns red after ticking the column, while the row is red for an open problem whether the column is shown or not. **Fix now:** the line rewritten after the «Тип замка» line the owner checked, with English and Hebrew.
- **F4** — Stale docs: ADR 0016 and 0017 still «accepted (planned, stage 6)»; five `CONTEXT.md` entries marked «planned»; `docs/project-spec.md` stage 6 promised «dots for done stages» (dropped at the check of ticket 6.5); spec § 4 still read as the live design although idea 006 replaced its colours and legend. **Fix now:** ADR statuses «accepted», the marks removed, the stage line updated, a «Changed» note at the top of spec § 4 pointing to idea 006.
- **F5** — A database whose rules are older than ticket 6.2 cannot read `pool_delayReasons`, `pool_culprits`, `pool_problems` (no catch-all read rule): no merge, no seeding, and the «правила базы устарели» toast of spec § 2.4 / § 8 never shows, because it is tied to a rejected write; a project created there has empty delay reasons and culprits. The person sees only «⬇ ошибка» naming the three collections. **Defer `KI-8`:** only a colleague with their own database who has not pasted the new rules (the owner's database has them); the fix touches the sync core and cannot be checked in `?mem=1`.
